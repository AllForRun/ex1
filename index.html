🚌 Transportation & Directions
From Seoul:

Take Sinchon/Hongdae Bus #3000 or Gimpo Goldline to Gurae Station -> Bus #60-3 / #70 to Ganghwa Bus Terminal.

From Incheon Airport (ICN):

Take Bus #308 from ICN Terminal 1 to Gimpo -> Transfer to Ganghwa-bound Bus #70 or #90.

📞 Emergency & Tourist Hotline
1330 Korea Travel Hotline

Call 1330 (Korea Travel Hotline) - Free 24/7 translation support in English, Japanese, Mandarin, and Russian.

Police: 112 | Ambulance/Fire: 119
💳 Practical Tips
Public Wi-Fi & Currency:

Free Public Wi-Fi 'Public WiFi Free' is available at major tourist spots & Ganghwa Bus Terminal.

Credit cards accepted almost everywhere. Small food vendors in traditional markets accept cash.
